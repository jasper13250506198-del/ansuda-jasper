SERVICE OVERVIEW

A practical plan starts with the cargo

Choose a full container when cargo volume, control or loading requirements justify dedicated equipment, or use LCL when your shipment can share container space. Jasper can review the cargo details, supplier location and Singapore delivery point before discussing a suitable arrangement.

FCL for larger consignments

Dedicated-container planning for commercial shipments, including discussion of equipment type, loading method and supplier pickup.

LCL for smaller volumes

Consolidation can suit cartons, crates and pallets that do not require a full container. Dimensions and packing details are important.

Origin coordination in China

Share the supplier city, cargo-ready date and loading conditions so pickup or warehouse delivery requirements can be reviewed.

Singapore clearance and delivery

Discuss documents, consignee details, delivery postcode, site access and unloading needs before the shipment is arranged.
