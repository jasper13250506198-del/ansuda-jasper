SERVICE PLANNING

Choosing between FCL and LCL

Sea freight is often considered for commercial cargo that is too large or cost-sensitive for air transport. The useful decision is not simply “sea or air”: importers need to compare a dedicated full container with consolidated LCL space, while accounting for packing, origin handling and delivery access in Singapore.

When FCL may be practical

A full container can suit larger consignments, cargo that benefits from dedicated space, or purchase orders that can be loaded together. Equipment type still depends on packed size, weight and loading conditions.

When LCL may be practical

LCL can suit smaller lots that are properly packed for shared-container handling. Each package must be measured because charges and handling are based on the shipment’s packed volume and weight.

Multiple suppliers in China

Collection or consolidation can be discussed when goods come from different factories. Provide every supplier address, contact and cargo-ready date so the origin plan can be reviewed.

Delivery in Singapore

A postcode is only the starting point. State whether delivery is to a warehouse, commercial building or project site, and disclose vehicle, loading-bay, timing and unloading constraints.

IMPORTER CHECKLIST

What Singapore importers should prepare

A clearer packing list and delivery brief helps Jasper compare the available scope without relying on assumptions.

Commercial documents

Prepare a commercial invoice and packing list showing clear product descriptions, quantities, package count, values and consignee details. Additional documents depend on the cargo.

Packing measurements

Record the final outer dimensions and gross weight of every pallet, crate or carton. Product dimensions alone are not enough for freight planning.

Scope boundaries

Confirm whether the enquiry includes China pickup, export handling, Singapore clearance support, duties or taxes, and final delivery. The quotation should state what is and is not included.

Before requesting a final quotation

Use final export-packed measurements and disclose the actual goods, supplier locations and delivery conditions. Charges, documentation and available handling depend on the shipment details, applicable regulations and the confirmed service scope.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Sea Freight from China to Singapore FAQ

These answers explain the information needed to review an enquiry. Final requirements depend on the actual shipment.

Should I use FCL or LCL for my shipment?

The answer depends on packed volume, weight, cargo type, handling risk, supplier timing and delivery requirements. Send the final packing data so Jasper can compare the practical options.

Can cargo from several Chinese suppliers be consolidated?

It can be discussed. Provide each pickup address, supplier contact, package details and cargo-ready date. The proposed origin handling will depend on the locations and timing.

What measurements are needed for an LCL quote?

Provide the length, width, height and gross weight of every packed piece, pallet or crate. Measurements should include export packaging, not only the product itself.

What documents should a Singapore importer prepare?

A commercial invoice and packing list are commonly needed, together with correct consignee and product information. The exact documentary requirements depend on the goods and shipment arrangement.

Can delivery to my Singapore address be included?

Delivery can be included in the requested scope, subject to cargo and access review. Send the complete address or postcode plus loading-bay, vehicle, timing and unloading information.
