SERVICE OVERVIEW

A practical plan starts with the cargo

Machinery cannot be quoted reliably from weight alone. Packed dimensions, centre of gravity, lifting points, photos, model information and loading or unloading conditions can affect equipment choice and handling. Share the technical cargo details before a route is proposed.

Packed-size assessment

Measure the final crate, skid or frame. State gross weight for each piece rather than only the combined shipment weight.

Lifting and loading details

Identify lifting points, forklift access, centre-of-gravity concerns and whether the supplier can load the selected equipment.

Standard or special equipment

Depending on packed dimensions and handling requirements, standard containers or other equipment may need to be discussed.

Singapore delivery conditions

Provide site access, floor loading, crane or forklift arrangements and unloading responsibility for practical delivery planning.
