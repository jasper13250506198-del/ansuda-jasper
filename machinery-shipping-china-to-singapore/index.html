SERVICE PLANNING

Machinery cannot be planned from total weight alone

Industrial equipment may be heavy, top-heavy, sensitive to tilting or too large for standard handling. Importers should obtain a packed-unit specification from the manufacturer before comparing container, handling and delivery options.

Final packed dimensions

Measure each machine after it is fixed to its skid, frame or crate. Include projections, supports and protective packing in the outer dimensions.

Weight and centre of gravity

State the gross weight of every unit and disclose known centre-of-gravity or stability concerns. Combined shipment weight is not enough for lifting planning.

Loading and lifting method

Identify forklift pockets, certified lifting points where provided by the manufacturer, crane needs and whether the supplier can load the proposed equipment.

Destination handling

Describe the Singapore site, access route, floor or ground conditions and available forklift, crane or rigging resources. Freight delivery should not be confused with positioning or installation.

IMPORTER CHECKLIST

Create a machinery shipping specification

One technical sheet per unit allows the forwarder to review equipment and handling assumptions before the cargo is collected.

Machine identification

Include equipment name, model, serial or purchase reference where appropriate, country of manufacture and a clear commercial description.

Shipping condition

State whether fluids are drained, batteries are disconnected or removed, moving parts are secured and the unit is protected against movement. Disclose remaining oils, fuels, batteries or pressure systems.

Handling drawings and photos

Provide photos of the packed unit and manufacturer lifting or securing instructions when available. Visual information supports—but does not replace—accurate dimensions and weights.

Before requesting a final quotation

Use final export-packed measurements and disclose the actual goods, supplier locations and delivery conditions. Charges, documentation and available handling depend on the shipment details, applicable regulations and the confirmed service scope.

WORK WITH ACCURATE DATA

Avoid assumptions in the quotation

Product photos or purchase values alone are not enough for freight planning. Measurements, weights and descriptions should match the final packed shipment. Jasper can review the information and explain the proposed scope, but customs, cargo acceptance and delivery arrangements remain subject to the actual goods and applicable requirements.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Machinery Shipping from China to Singapore FAQ

These answers explain the information needed to review an enquiry. Final requirements depend on the actual shipment.

When is machinery considered oversized?

That depends on the final packed dimensions, weight, available equipment and route constraints. Send the complete packed specification so the practical options can be reviewed.

What lifting information should the manufacturer provide?

Request the gross weight per unit, centre-of-gravity information where available, forklift pockets, lifting points and manufacturer handling instructions.

Do oils, batteries or other machine contents need to be disclosed?

Yes. Disclose any fuel or oil residue, batteries, pressurised systems, magnets, chemicals or other special characteristics before the shipment is planned.

Can delivery include unloading at my Singapore facility?

Unloading can be discussed only after the site and equipment requirements are known. Provide access dimensions, ground or floor conditions and available forklift, crane or rigging resources.

What documents help describe imported machinery?

A detailed commercial invoice, packing list, model or technical information and accurate consignee details are useful. Additional requirements depend on the equipment and applicable rules.
