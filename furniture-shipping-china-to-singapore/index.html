SERVICE OVERVIEW

A practical plan starts with the cargo

Furniture shipments vary widely in volume, fragility, packing and site access. A useful quotation starts with supplier locations, packing lists, package dimensions and delivery conditions—not only a product photo or purchase value.

FCL and LCL selection

A full container may suit larger projects or multi-item orders, while LCL can be considered for smaller, well-packed consignments.

Supplier consolidation

If goods come from several Chinese suppliers, share every pickup city and cargo-ready schedule so consolidation requirements can be assessed.

Packing and protection

Confirm whether items are flat-packed, crated or fully assembled. Fragile finishes, glass and stone elements need clear identification.

Singapore site access

Provide the postcode plus lift, loading-bay, vehicle, timing and manpower constraints. Final-mile scope depends on actual access conditions.
