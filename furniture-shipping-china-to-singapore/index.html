SERVICE PLANNING

Furniture freight begins with packing and access

Furniture orders can be light but bulky, fragile at exposed edges, or difficult to deliver when fully assembled. Importers should collect final packing details from suppliers and describe the destination site before choosing LCL, FCL or a consolidation plan.

Flat-packed versus assembled

Flat-packed items may use container space more efficiently, while assembled pieces can need more protection and handling room. Quote using export-packed measurements.

Fragile surfaces and components

Identify glass, stone, mirrors, polished finishes and projecting parts. Ask the supplier to describe the protective packing rather than assuming standard cartons are sufficient.

Multi-supplier purchasing

For furniture sourced from several factories, list every pickup location and ready date. Consolidation feasibility depends on geography, timing, package data and warehouse handling.

Last-mile site conditions

Advise whether delivery is to a landed property, warehouse, retail unit or project site. Lift size, loading-bay height, stairs and unloading labour can affect the delivery scope.

IMPORTER CHECKLIST

A useful furniture packing schedule

Create one line per packed item or package group so volume, handling and supplier readiness can be reviewed.

Item and package references

Match each furniture description to its carton, crate or bundle count. Include final outer dimensions, gross weight and supplier name.

Material disclosure

Identify solid wood, engineered wood, upholstery, glass, stone or metal components accurately. Documentary or import requirements may vary by material.

Receiving plan

Confirm the delivery address, access route and who will unload or move items beyond the delivery point. Do not assume installation or upstairs handling is included.

Before requesting a final quotation

Use final export-packed measurements and disclose the actual goods, supplier locations and delivery conditions. Charges, documentation and available handling depend on the shipment details, applicable regulations and the confirmed service scope.

WORK WITH ACCURATE DATA

Avoid assumptions in the quotation

Product photos or purchase values alone are not enough for freight planning. Measurements, weights and descriptions should match the final packed shipment. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Furniture Shipping from China to Singapore FAQ

These answers explain the information needed to review an enquiry. Final requirements depend on the actual shipment.

Can furniture from different Chinese suppliers ship together?

Consolidation can be assessed when every supplier address, ready date and packing list is available. The practical plan depends on distance, timing, volume and warehouse handling.

Is LCL suitable for a small furniture order?

It may be, if the furniture is packed to withstand shared-container handling and the package data is accurate. Fragility, package size and final delivery requirements must also be considered.

What packing information should I request from the supplier?

Ask for the final outer dimensions and gross weight of each package, the packing method, material description and photos of completed export packing.

What should I disclose about the Singapore delivery site?

Provide the address or postcode, property type, loading-bay or vehicle limits, lift dimensions, stairs, receiving hours and who will handle unloading.

Does a freight quotation include furniture assembly or installation?

Do not assume it does. Assembly, installation and movement beyond the agreed delivery point should be discussed separately and stated explicitly if offered in the quotation.
