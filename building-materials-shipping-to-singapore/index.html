SERVICE OVERVIEW

A practical plan starts with the cargo

Building and renovation cargo often combines dense, fragile, long or irregularly packed items. Early information about each commodity, package, supplier and job-site constraint helps determine whether LCL, FCL or a cargo-specific handling plan should be discussed.

Hardware and fittings

Commercial quantities of fittings, tools, lighting components and related supplies can be reviewed from packing lists and product descriptions.

Tiles and fragile materials

Dense or breakable cargo needs suitable packing and accurate pallet or crate information before shipping options are assessed.

Long and bulky pieces

Panels, profiles, doors and other long items should be measured in their final export packing, including any support frames.

Project delivery planning

Provide the Singapore postcode, delivery window, vehicle access and unloading arrangements so the requested scope is clear.
