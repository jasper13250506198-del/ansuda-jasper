HOW IS SEA FREIGHT VOLUME CALCULATED?

CBM uses packed outer dimensions

Bulky cargo shipping charges are calculated based on cubic metres (CBM).

CBM = Length (CM) × Width (CM) × Height (CM) ÷ 1,000,000.

Measurements must be based on the external dimensions of the fully packed cargo.

Final chargeable volume is determined by actual measurements taken at our warehouse. Dimensions provided by suppliers or customers are for reference only.

SINGAPORE IMPORT GST

GST is charged separately

Singapore's prevailing import GST rate is 9%, where applicable.

Shipping rates include customs clearance and standard islandwide door-to-door delivery in Singapore. Import GST is charged separately.

Import GST is calculated based on the applicable customs value in accordance with Singapore Customs regulations. The customs value can include costs incidental to the sale and delivery of the goods into Singapore; it is not promised to be based only on the declared goods value.

Accurate declarations are required

Customers must accurately declare the value and description of their goods. Import GST collected on behalf of Singapore Customs is not revenue earned by ANSUDA Logistics. The calculator does not automatically estimate an unconfirmed GST amount.

PROHIBITED & CONTROLLED GOODS

Cargo acceptance requires regulatory review

The lists below state ANSUDA's no-carry policy and common categories that may require permits. They do not replace the legal classification of a specific product, and some legal rules can contain definitions or exceptions.

ANSUDA does not carry

  • Endangered wildlife and restricted wildlife products
  • Chewing gum
  • Firecrackers
  • Chewing tobacco
  • E-cigarettes and vaping products
  • Controlled drugs
  • Handguns and revolvers

This is a company acceptance policy. It should not be read as a claim that every listed term has no statutory exception in every context.

May require a permit or approval

  • Processed food containing meat
  • Fresh fruits and vegetables
  • Baby food
  • Plants and plant products
  • Radio communication equipment
  • Medicines and pharmaceutical products

Whether a permit or approval is required depends on the goods, quantity and applicable rules. Controlled goods must not be imported without the required approval.

Sensitive cargo: Every item must comply with Singapore import law and transport requirements. The same rate for accepted general and sensitive cargo does not mean every sensitive item is accepted.
