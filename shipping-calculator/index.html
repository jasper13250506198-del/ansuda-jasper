HOW SEA FREIGHT VOLUME IS CALCULATED

Use fully packed outer dimensions

Bulky cargo shipping charges are calculated based on cubic metres (CBM).

CBM = Length (CM) × Width (CM) × Height (CM) ÷ 1,000,000.

Small-parcel sea freight: for each package, compare actual and volumetric weight (L × W × H ÷ 6000), use the greater value, add all package chargeable weights, then round the shipment total up to a whole KG.

Air freight: calculate total actual weight and total volumetric weight separately, use the greater shipment total, then round up to a whole KG.

Measurements must be based on the external dimensions of the fully packed cargo. Final chargeable volume is determined by actual measurements taken at our warehouse. Dimensions provided by suppliers or customers are for reference only.
