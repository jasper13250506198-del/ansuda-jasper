SERVICE OVERVIEW

A practical plan starts with the cargo

Air freight can be considered when delivery timing matters more than the lower unit cost commonly associated with sea freight. The right plan depends on chargeable weight, dimensions, commodity, packing, origin airport or pickup city, and delivery requirements in Singapore.

Chargeable-weight review

Air freight is assessed using actual and volumetric weight. Accurate package dimensions help avoid misleading estimates.

Commodity and packing check

Provide a clear cargo description and packing method. Batteries, liquids, magnets and other controlled items require early disclosure.

China pickup coordination

Supplier pickup can be discussed based on the collection address, cargo-ready time and handover requirements.

Arrival and local delivery

Customs documentation, consignee information and the final Singapore delivery point should be prepared before dispatch.
