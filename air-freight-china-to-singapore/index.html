SERVICE PLANNING

When air freight deserves consideration

Air freight is usually evaluated when shipment timing and inventory needs justify a faster transport mode for suitable goods. A meaningful comparison requires the actual gross weight and the volumetric size of the final packages, along with enough product information to check cargo acceptance requirements.

Actual and volumetric weight

Airfreight quotations use chargeable weight, which considers both scale weight and package volume. The applicable calculation basis should be confirmed in the quotation.

Commodity disclosure

Use a specific product description rather than “parts” or “samples”. Disclose batteries, liquids, powders, magnets, pressurised items or other special characteristics before booking.

Pickup or supplier handover

Provide the China collection address and cargo-ready time. If the supplier will deliver to a nominated point, clarify the handover location and required labels or documents.

Arrival and delivery scope

State whether you need airport-related handling, Singapore clearance support and local delivery. Include the postcode and access requirements for the final address.

IMPORTER CHECKLIST

Build an air-cargo data sheet

Accurate package-level data reduces the risk of a quotation changing when cargo is measured at handover.

Package-level dimensions

List the outer length, width and height of each carton or crate after packing. If packages differ, separate them by size and quantity.

Product information

Share the product name, material or use, model information where relevant, and any battery or liquid details. Photos can support—but should not replace—a written description.

Importer and delivery data

Prepare consignee information and the Singapore delivery postcode. Mention receiving hours, loading-bay restrictions or appointments required by the destination.

Before requesting a final quotation

Use final export-packed measurements and disclose the actual goods, supplier locations and delivery conditions. Charges, documentation and available handling depend on the shipment details, applicable regulations and the confirmed service scope.

WORK WITH ACCURATE DATA

Avoid assumptions in the quotation

Product photos or purchase values alone are not enough for freight planning. Measurements, weights and descriptions should match the final packed shipment. Jasper can review the information and explain the proposed scope, but customs, cargo acceptance and delivery arrangements remain subject to the actual goods and applicable requirements.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Air Freight from China to Singapore FAQ

These answers explain the information needed to review an enquiry. Final requirements depend on the actual shipment.

How is chargeable weight different from gross weight?

Gross weight is the scale weight. Chargeable weight also considers the space occupied by the packages. The carrier’s applicable volumetric basis should be confirmed when the shipment is quoted.

What cargo details must be disclosed before air shipping?

Give a precise commodity description and disclose batteries, liquids, powders, magnets, pressurised contents or other special characteristics. Incomplete descriptions can prevent a reliable acceptance review.

Can Jasper arrange pickup from my supplier in China?

Pickup can be discussed as part of the quotation. Provide the full collection address, contact person, cargo-ready date, package count and any handover restrictions.

Can I request delivery to my office or warehouse in Singapore?

Yes, include it in the enquiry. Provide the postcode, floor or loading-bay information, receiving hours and any appointment rules so the delivery scope can be assessed.

How should I compare air freight with sea freight?

Compare the complete service scope, chargeable quantity, cargo suitability and business timing—not only a headline freight figure. Jasper can review both options when the shipment data is complete.
